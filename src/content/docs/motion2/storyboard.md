## Storyboards

A storyboard is not just a **drawing**.  
Use the **script** and a **description** of your scene to paint the complete picture.

### Why storyboarding?

Very important part of the production process.

A storyboard, like a wireframe for a website, is a must for film/video with several scenes.

### Advantages

**Framing**  
What should be visible in the image?

**Camera shots**  
Storyboards can show the intended pan, tilt or zoom of a camera.

**Editing**  
Shows the editing possibilities where scenes merge into each other. For example, using a path cut or transitions.

## From concept to pitch

### Pitch pdf

What are the contents of a pitch pdf in the context of motion?

- (Customer / Product)
- (Script)
- Style board
- Motion board
- Storyboard
- Style frames
- Extra: descriptions, suggestions,...

#### 1. Style board

It is very important to communicate your vision of a project to a client.  
Clients cannot always empathise with your proposal.

Acts as a reference for you as a designer or colleaques.

**Indispensable** for any design project!

#### 2. Motion board

Similar to a style board but specifically for animation / timing / effects / transitions.  
**Not to copy > to inpsire**.

#### 3. Style frames

Style frames are a visual representation of what a motion project or film could become before it is animated.

It is a frame or image that represents the look and feel of the project. These help in winning pitches for commercial projects.

**Look & Feel**  
The purpose of a style frame is to create a unique look and feel that visually holds a motion design project together.

**Stylistic Guides**  
Style frames help direct the efforts of a production team. They serve as visual guides by defining the creative boundaries of a project: colour, typography, texture, camera placements, etc.

**Concept is King**  
Style frames should be visually striking, but more importantly, they should reflect a concept. Don't let them be just eye-candy.

#### 4. Animatic

An animatic is a series of storyboard frames timed into a pre-visualisation video to give a preview of the final video.  
The detail of the animatic depends on the budget. some are already animated.  
They are not always necesary, but they give a more comprehensive picture of the entire project. Usually with a first version of soundtrack and/or voice-over.

**Timesaver in the long run**!

- Usually created with an initial version of the soundtrack and/or voice-over.
- Helps visually plan the story before investing time in full animation or editing.
- Allows you to see how scenes connect and whether transitions feel logical.
- Makes it possible to test scene timing, motion, and audio.
- Reveals whether scenes move too fast or too slowly, so pacing can be adjusted early before entering the more intensive motion design phase.
- Ideal for presentations or gathering feedback from a client or team.
